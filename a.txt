For all its impressive size, however, the compute market lacks sophistication. The
“hyperscalers” (a still somewhat arcane piece of IT jargon for a handful of giant
cloud-computing providers including Amazon, Google and Microsoft) and frontier AI labs
(such as Anthropic and OpenAI) make deals for tens or even hundreds of billions of dollars of
compute. But such arrangements are unavailable to thousands of smaller firms. Buyers and
sellers also have few of the derivatives that in established markets allow them to hedge their
exposures and speculate.

然而，尽管算力市场的规模令人印象深刻，但它缺乏成熟度。所谓的“超大规模企业”（hyperscalers，这是IT界一个仍有些晦涩的行话，指代少数几家大型云计算提供商，包括亚马逊、谷歌和微软）以及前沿AI实验室（如Anthropic和OpenAI），动辄达成数百亿甚至上千亿美元的算力交易。但成千上万家较小的公司却无法获得这种级别的合作。此外，买家和卖家也几乎没有衍生品可用——而在成熟市场中，衍生品能让他们对冲风险敞口并进行投机
